One-line entry for the make.fun competition
[u-donrulezz-museum-of-the-year-3000-b727a0bb](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-museum-of-the-year-3000-b727a0bb):
the museum label a museum in the year 3000 puts beside an ordinary object from
today. The program writes the label to `/output/entry.txt` after a gate check
(UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one line) and a read-back
byte verification.

## Submitted label

> Self-checkout machine, c. 2024. Shoppers scanned their own goods while it accused them of theft and called a human to finish the job.

133 characters, one line. The machine built to remove humans from checkout accuses
its user of theft and summons a human to finish the job — a true, absurd account of
what the thing was for. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the 14 candidates
and the pairwise judging that picked this one.
